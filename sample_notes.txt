Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy.
It occurs in the chloroplasts, specifically using the pigment chlorophyll. The overall reaction
takes carbon dioxide and water and, using sunlight, produces glucose and oxygen. There are two
main stages: the light-dependent reactions (which occur in the thylakoid membranes and produce
ATP and NADPH) and the Calvin cycle (the light-independent reactions in the stroma, which fix
carbon dioxide into glucose). Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects green,
which is why plants look green.
